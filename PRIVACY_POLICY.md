# Privacy Policy — Bizlance

**Effective date:** [INSERT DATE]

Bizlance ("Bizlance," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website bizlance.online and connects
businesses with vetted AI service providers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we
collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly**
- Contact details submitted through forms on our site (e.g. name, email, company name, message content)
- Account information if you register with Bizlance (e.g. business name, email, service category)

**Information from our social media accounts (Facebook & Instagram)**
Bizlance operates its own Facebook Page and Instagram Business account. Through Meta's Graph API, we:
- Publish our own marketing content to these accounts
- Read comments and engagement on our own posts (such as commenter usernames and comment text) in order to prepare replies from our team

We only access data on accounts we own and administer. We do not access, read, or post on behalf of any other person's or business's Facebook or Instagram account.

**Automatically collected information**
- Standard web log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) collected via our hosting/analytics tools

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:
- Operate and improve Bizlance's platform and matchmaking service
- Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
- Publish and manage our own social media presence
- Prepare (human-reviewed) replies to comments and messages on our own accounts
- Send outreach communications to businesses, where permitted (see Section 5)
- Monitor and maintain platform security

## 3. What We Do Not Do

- We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.
- We do **not** share data collected via the Meta Graph API with any third party outside of operating Bizlance's own marketing.
- We do **not** use automated tools to send unsolicited direct messages on Instagram or Facebook.

## 4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with:
- Service providers who help us operate Bizlance (e.g. email delivery, hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality obligations
- Legal or regulatory authorities, only where required by law

## 5. Email Communications

If we contact your business via email as part of outreach, every message includes a clear way to opt out. Requesting removal via reply or unsubscribe link will stop future emails from us. We retain a suppression list solely to honor these requests.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.
